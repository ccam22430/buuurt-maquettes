# COM-PUB-SPON-003 geodesic targeting

The [founder's decision](https://github.com/ccam22430/buuurt-maquettes/issues/17#issuecomment-7240000082) supersedes cycling estimates and isochrones. The live review paths only load the official Amsterdam boundaries and a fixture file containing the existing geocoded shop centres plus stable demo inventory point IDs and coordinates. Previously generated cycling evidence and its scripts remain in the repository for archival replay and verification.

The model uses the haversine great-circle metric on the IUGG mean Earth radius (6,371,008.8 m). Geographic rings are generated by the corresponding direct geodesic formula and clipped against the exact municipal geometries (CC0) using locally served polygon-clipping 0.15.7 (MIT, license in vendor directory). The rendered circle chord approximation has at most 0.2 m sagitta. The commercial model computes distances directly from the two shop coordinates, independently of the rendered rings. Across very large distances or when centimetre-level WGS84 accuracy matters, use an ellipsoidal implementation. The static prototype uses Amsterdam geometries; its rural profile tests defaults and overrides without expanding geographic coverage.

Default urban radii are 1/3 km and rural radii 5/10 km, inclusive at each boundary. A business override wins over a city setting, then the applicable profile default. Current staff review can edit the Amsterdam city setting, individual reference stores, and a rural test profile. An approved district or global extension acts on the sponsored outer band only. Overlapping applicable grants use the maximum outer limit. Merchant selection uses distinct standard and extended IDs within one district. Green remains included and the color of any portion is independent of selection.

Legacy approvals with no explicit radius keep their original status, criteria and audit trail. The merchant receives a notice, and Super Admin can record a new radius without discarding the former approval. No timed permission is automatically translated into metres or silently used for a new radial purchase. Engaged campaigns and financial snapshots remain intact. Existing tier-specific quote, inventory locking, surcharge, margin, quota and commercial guarantees stay in place. Synthetic inventory still uses the same physical point/day keys, now classified by distance rather than estimated network time. The scanned shop's registered coordinates and official district are the inputs for the eligibility function. Integration with the real feed, real partner inventory and actual QR services is outside the static review. No resident location is collected.

Automated checks cover distance, exact inclusive boundaries, clipping, separate selection segments and inventory, scope precedence, and explicit renewal of legacy permissions.
